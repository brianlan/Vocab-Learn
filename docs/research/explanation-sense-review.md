# Explanation sense selection

Review date: 2026-11-04. The learning definition is authored source material. Generator prompts should illustrate that meaning; they should not silently replace it with another sense that seems more common.

The current 1,645-card snapshot was read for source and bilingual-example quality. Suspected sense-selection problems received targeted checks against primary learner dictionaries. This is a practical commonness screen, not a corpus-frequency ranking of every sense of every word. A dictionary's first entry, an unlabelled sense, or a CEFR label alone does not establish the most frequent meaning.

## Selection rule

For an unqualified headword, prefer a contemporary, broadly useful learner sense with ordinary examples. Record the intended word class or construction clearly. A standard everyday or academic meaning is a reasonable choice even when another common meaning exists. Formal does not automatically mean rare. Distinguish an unfamiliar word from a familiar word taught in an unnecessarily marginal sense.

An explicitly intended inflected form, phrase, technical sense or secondary meaning can be legitimate. Make that scope clear rather than automatically lemmatizing it or forcing every word into its first dictionary definition. Multiple common senses are a coverage choice; putting all of them into one definition is not required to make a single-sense card correct.

## Utility

The public-service meaning is common and worth retaining. Oxford lists public supplies such as electricity, water and gas; Cambridge groups the service and its supplying business under the same service heading. The existing provider-company example is consequently ordinary usage, rather than a demonstrated wrong-sense generation. An earlier audit flag treating the service/company distinction as a definite error was withdrawn.

Usefulness is another established meaning, marked formal by Oxford. Computing utilities are another sense. These references do not establish a numerical frequency ranking, and do not justify replacing public services with usefulness simply because the latter feels more fundamental. See [Oxford's utility entry](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/utility_1) and [Cambridge's utility entry](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/utility).

A possible clarification, without changing the selected sense, is: “A utility is a public service, such as water or electricity, or a company that provides such a service.” This is a proposed source wording, not an applied database edit.

## Source correctness versus sense preference

The separate source review found scoped issues concerning allergy/intolerance, a hunting requirement added to carnivore, categorical dinosaur size, minute/second in an exact-unit definition, multiplication/product, renewable-resource qualification, and the article/countability in A honour. These concern source accuracy or wording, rather than selecting an obscure sense. They should be addressed as source maintenance, not hidden with Generator instructions.

The supporting checks distinguish [food intolerance and allergy](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/food-intolerance/), define [carnivore by diet](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/carnivore), allow [small as well as large dinosaurs](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/dinosaur), specify [one minute as sixty seconds](https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-811/nist-guide-si-chapter-5-units-outside-si), and distinguish [multiplication](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/multiplication) from its [product](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/product). Renewable-resource wording needs qualification for stocks consumed faster than replenishment; the [IPCC's introductory renewable-energy chapter](https://www.ipcc.ch/site/assets/uploads/2018/03/Chapter-1-Renewable-Energy-and-Climate-Change-1.pdf), section 1.2, makes that distinction. This does not make the current solar-energy example false.

Several initial suspicions were dismissed after reference checks: ordinary melt/dissolve senses overlap; introverted can have the supplied learner meaning; knockout has a blow sense; constituent/选民 and slip/滑倒 are documented ordinary equivalents. Technical distinctions do not automatically invalidate an ordinary dictionary sense. Keep qualified scientific/legal scope notes separate from actual learning errors.

No explanation, example, learning progress or source version was changed by this review. The private per-card ledger and primary-source verification preserve the initial flags, their adjudication and their limits.
